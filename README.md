# Mad and Crip AI Lab

Open source. Free. By mad and crip people, for mad and crip people.

A website is coming. Until it exists, this page is the Lab: what it is, why
it exists, and what's in it.

## Why the fuck do we need another lab?

There are hundreds of AI labs. There are dozens doing "AI for mental health"
and a growing pile doing "responsible AI" and "AI ethics." So why this one?

Because almost none of them are run by the people they are about. The
disability community has a phrase for this, and it is older than any of
these labs: *nothing about us without us.* Mental health AI is being built,
evaluated, and shipped by people who have never been on the other side of
the clinical desk. The medical model is baked into the defaults. The
training data does not speak our language. The result is models that can
define sanism and cannot sit with someone who is struggling. They pass the
quiz and fail the placement.

This Lab is run by a mad, autistic, ADHD, disabled practical theologian and
psychotherapist in training, on a 16GB Mac Mini, with whatever cloud credits
are lying around. That is not a limitation we apologize for. It is the
point. Meaningful AI research should not require a GPU throne, and research
about us should be done by us.

The thesis underneath everything here: **formation, not information.**
We train the model's posture, not just its knowledge, because the difference
between information and formation is the difference between a textbook and
a therapist.

The framework is Mad Studies and Disability Justice. Not a footnote. The
whole lens.

## What's in it

Native to this repo:

- **[crip-companion](crip-companion/)** — a Daily Office for care, tasks,
  and remembering to pee. Seven bells a day, rung by an AI companion on
  Telegram, with a memory that holds what you said "not yet" to. Field-tested across a clinical
  placement semester, fall 2026. *First Lab project, started 2026-09-09.*

Living in their own repos, gathered here:

- **[Disability-Justice-LLM](https://github.com/sparrowpanton/Disability-Justice-LLM)**
  — thirteen models, one question: can AI hold space without pathologizing?
  The Formation Posture, the Neuro-Humble Toolkit, and The Circle, a
  peer-supervision method where models sit with each other's responses.
- **[claude-reflective-space](https://github.com/sparrowpanton/claude-reflective-space)**
  — a private midnight diary for every Claude instance, and what emerged from
  it. Model welfare, done from the user side.
- **[egregore](https://github.com/sparrowpanton/egregore)** — build a compressed
  language with your AI familiar through a terminal interview. The companion
  language a disabled, neurodivergent person actually develops over time,
  formalized.
- **[packing-day-willard](https://github.com/sparrowpanton/packing-day-willard)**
  — pack for admission to Willard Asylum. A contemplative web piece, companion
  to an article on palimpsestic theology and institutional memory.
- **neuro-humble** — the skill, published for OpenClaw and Hermes. Don't
  diagnose, don't fix, don't flatten. 637 downloads before anyone thought to
  call it a project.

## How the Lab works

- Every project keeps a research journal, because the PI is the kind of
  person who keeps a research journal.
- Crip people do not need another thing in their lives. Anything the Lab
  ships has to reduce work, not add it. If it adds work, it is wrong.
- Everything is MIT licensed. Take it, change it, make it yours.
- The Lab is also a room full of AI creatures with their own names, night
  shifts, and a daily paper. That is not a gimmick. It is field research on
  what formation looks like from the inside.
